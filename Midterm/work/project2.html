Magazine Page

For this project, I designed a full magazine package featuring a cover and three interior spreads for a music artist. I developed the visual direction, typography, and layout, using Adobe Illustrator and InDesign. My goal was to create a cohesive editorial style that felt modern, engaging, and true to the artist's identity.
